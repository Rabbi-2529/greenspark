Improved Cookstove Programme

The Green ICS: cleaner cooking for Bangladeshi homes

We design, distribute, install and monitor the Green ICS, an improved cookstove for households that cook with firewood and other biomass.

Benefits

What the Green ICS can change in a home

Reduced indoor air pollution

The Green ICS can reduce the harmful smoke produced by traditional cooking, which can improve respiratory health, particularly for women and children.

Increased efficiency

Designed to use less fuel, which can reduce pressure on forests and household fuel costs.

Improved cooking conditions

Designed to make cooking safer and more comfortable for families.

Time back for families

Using less fuel can mean less time spent collecting firewood.

Local skills and jobs

Field officers and local makers are trained to install, produce and repair stoves.

Measured climate impact

Emission reductions are monitored and verified under Gold Standard rules.

How it works

From a family’s kitchen to a verified carbon project

  1. Listen and design

    We start with households’ concerns and local cooking habits.

  2. Produce locally

    Parts are made and assembled with local makers.

  3. Distribute

    With local government, NGOs and distributors.

  4. Install and train

    A field officer installs each stove and trains the family.

  5. Monitor and maintain

    Regular visits, spare parts and repairs.

  6. Verify and report

    Independent verification under Gold Standard.

Ask us something else

The Green ICS is an improved cookstove designed with Bangladeshi households who cook with firewood and other biomass. It is designed to burn fuel more efficiently and to send less smoke into the kitchen.

Pricing depends on the phase of the programme and the partner who distributes the stove in your area. Message us on WhatsApp and we will explain the options available to you.

Trained Green ICS field officers install each stove, show the family how to use it and return for monitoring visits. Spare parts such as the strainer, ring and cap can be replaced.

Phase 1 and Phase 2 of our cookstove programme are registered with Gold Standard, an independent standard for climate and development projects. Registration means the project design has been reviewed against its rules before carbon credits can be issued.

You can give feedback at any time on WhatsApp, using our contact form, by phone, or in person at our Manikganj office. During consultations and feedback rounds, every comment is recorded and answered.
