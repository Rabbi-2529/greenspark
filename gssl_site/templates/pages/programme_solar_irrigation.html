About the programme

Replacing diesel irrigation pumps with solar pumping, so farmers can water their fields without diesel.

The programme is at an early stage. We are working with communities, local government and technical partners to shape it, and will publish more detail as it develops.

Programme stage

ConceptIdea shaped with communities and partners.
FeasibilityTechnical, social and carbon feasibility studies.
PilotA small pilot to test the approach in the field.
ActiveRunning at scale and monitored.

Solar instead of diesel

Solar pumps replace diesel pumps for irrigation.

Sized for smallholders

Pump sizes matched to the fields farmers already work.

Lower running costs

Sunlight replaces fuel purchases, which can lower the cost of irrigation.
