About Green Spark

We develop carbon projects and clean-energy programmes with communities in Bangladesh, Nepal and Cambodia. Clean cooking, rooftop solar MRV and safe drinking water lead our work, designed to improve community health and reduce harm to the environment.

Our head office is in Rockdale, NSW, Australia. Our operations base is in Manikganj, Bangladesh, where our field team works with households across the programme districts.

Clean cooking

The Green ICS is an improved cookstove designed with Bangladeshi households who cook with firewood and other biomass. It is designed to burn fuel more efficiently and to send less smoke into the kitchen.

The stove is designed to burn wood more completely and to carry smoke out of the kitchen. This can reduce smoke indoors, the firewood a family needs and the time spent gathering it.

Trained field officers install each stove, show the family how to use it and return for monitoring visits. Spare parts such as the strainer, ring and cap can be replaced.

Carbon credits and Gold Standard

A carbon credit represents one tonne of carbon dioxide, or the equivalent in other greenhouse gases, reduced or avoided and verified under a recognised standard. Credits from our projects come from the change households make, such as cooking with an efficient stove.

Gold Standard is an independent standard for climate and development projects. Registration means the project design has been reviewed against its rules before carbon credits can be issued.

Phase 1 and Phase 2 of the Improved Cookstove Programme. Each has a public page on the Gold Standard Impact Registry: Phase 1 · GS13141 (opens in a new tab) and Phase 2 · GS23304 (opens in a new tab).

Field officers visit households, check that stoves are in use and in good condition, and keep a record for every installation. An independent verifier reviews the results before credits are issued.
