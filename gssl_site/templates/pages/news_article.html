How MRV works for rooftop solar

Measure, report, verify: how generation from many rooftops can become one carbon programme that buyers can check.

By the GSSL team12 November 20266 min read
{% endif %} {% if sections.article %}

Rooftop solar systems are small on their own. Brought together, they can add up to a carbon programme that buyers and investors can trust. The step that makes this possible is MRV: measurement, reporting and verification.

Measure

Each system's generation is recorded from its inverter or meter. The data is collected on a regular schedule, so gaps and errors are easy to spot.

Report

Data from many rooftops is brought together and reported against a documented method, so anyone can see how a result was worked out.

Verify

An independent verification body checks the method, the data and the records before any credits can be issued.

Where the programme stands

Our rooftop solar programme is in development. We will share more, including how system owners can take part, once each step is approved.

  • Clear records for every system.
  • One method, applied the same way to every rooftop.
  • Independent checks before any credit is issued.
