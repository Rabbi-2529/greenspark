What sets us apart

Six reasons partners work with Green Spark

Built with communities

We plan each programme with households, local government and NGOs, so that it fits local needs and lasts.

Held to recognised standards

Phase 1 and Phase 2 of our cookstove programme are registered with Gold Standard.

Monitoring you can check

Field officers visit households, check equipment and keep a clear record for every installation, ready for independent verification.

On the ground in three countries

Head office in Rockdale, NSW, an operations base in Manikganj, Bangladesh, and work in Nepal and Cambodia.

A pipeline beyond cookstoves

Safe drinking water, solar irrigation, rooftop solar, rural productive-use solar and low-emission rice programmes are in development.

Local skills and jobs

We train field officers and local makers to install, produce and repair equipment close to the families who use it.

How we work

From first conversation to verified result

  1. Listen and plan

    We start with the community’s needs and the local context.

  2. Design and test

    We design the programme and test it with local partners.

  3. Install and train

    Trained field officers install equipment and show families how to use it.

  4. Monitor and verify

    We return for monitoring visits and prepare records for independent verification.
