Making an impact

Green Spark is a carbon credit aggregator platform. We bring rooftop solar MRV, improved cookstoves and safe drinking water together into verified carbon programmes, so climate action spread across thousands of rooftops and households becomes credits that buyers and investors can trust.

Rooftop solar MRV leads our work, then our improved cookstove programme, with safe drinking water next in the pipeline. Our cookstove projects are registered with Gold Standard.
