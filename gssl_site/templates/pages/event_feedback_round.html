What is a feedback round?

After the local stakeholder consultation, Gold Standard projects keep the conversation open for at least 60 days. During this time anyone can ask about the project and send comments. We publish a summary of the feedback and how we have responded.

  1. Learn about the programme

    Read the programme page, or ask our team for a summary in Bangla or English.

  2. Share your views

    Send us a WhatsApp message, use the form, call or visit our office.

  3. We record and reply

    Every comment is logged and answered in the feedback report.
