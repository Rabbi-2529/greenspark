About this consultation

Gold Standard projects hold a local stakeholder consultation so that people affected by a project can learn about it and shape it before and during implementation. This meeting is open to households, local government officers, NGOs and anyone with an interest in the programme.

We will explain the programme in Bangla, answer questions and record every comment. A summary report, with our responses, is published on this page after the meeting.
