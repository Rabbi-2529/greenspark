Who we are

We are carbon project developers who work in Bangladesh, Nepal and Cambodia. With local communities, government agencies, NGOs and private-sector partners, we develop programmes in clean cooking, clean energy, safe water and low-emission farming.

Our approach

A holistic approach to every project

We focus on three things that make programmes last.

Community engagement

We work closely with local communities to understand their needs and preferences, so that our solutions are culturally appropriate and sustainable.

Partnerships

We collaborate with government agencies, NGOs and private-sector organisations, drawing on their expertise and resources to maximise our impact.

Research and development
